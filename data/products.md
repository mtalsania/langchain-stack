## Notes
- Every model ships with a 630 Wh battery except the Fold, which has a 360 Wh battery.
- A spare battery costs 549 USD (630 Wh) or 329 USD (360 Wh).
- The Cargo model needs a 2-hour assembly appointment, booked at checkout.
- Students get 8% off any bike with a valid student ID. Discounts do not stack.
