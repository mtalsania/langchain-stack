# Nimbus Bikes: Support and Shipping

## Support hours
- Chat and email: Monday to Saturday, 07:30 to 19:00 Central European Time.
- Phone support is only for warranty claims and is available on weekdays.
- Average first response time on tickets: 3 hours.

## Ticket priorities
- P1: rider safety issue (brakes, frame crack, battery swelling). Answered within 1 hour.
- P2: bike unusable but safe. Answered within 1 business day.
- P3: questions, accessories, billing. Answered within 2 business days.

## Shipping
- Free shipping on bikes inside the EU and the UK.
- Delivery takes 4 to 9 business days. Cargo bikes take up to 14 business days.
- Shipping outside the EU/UK is not available yet.
