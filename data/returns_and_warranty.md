# Nimbus Bikes: Returns and Warranty Policy

Nimbus Bikes is a fictional e-bike company used for this course. The numbers below are
deliberately unusual so you can tell when the model answers from these documents
instead of from its general knowledge.

## Returns
- Customers can return an unused bike within **37 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Bikes ridden more than **25 km** count as used and get a 15% restocking fee.
- Accessories (helmets, locks, lights) can be returned within 14 days, unopened only.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 6 business days.

## Warranty
- Frames: **9-year** warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Motor and battery: **3-year** warranty or 18,000 km, whichever comes first.
- The battery is considered defective if it holds less than 72% of its original capacity
  within the warranty period.
- Warranty does not cover crash damage, water submersion, or unofficial modifications
  (for example "derestricting" the motor speed limit).

## How to file a claim
Open a support ticket with your order number, a photo of the issue, and the odometer
reading. Claims are answered within 2 business days.
